# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site, chosen for speed, simple hosting, and low maintenance.

## Users

People and companies evaluating masonry blocks or construction materials, especially in Vrancea and the Focșani area, plus customers elsewhere in Romania who need delivery. They need to understand the offer quickly and reach the company by phone or WhatsApp in one or two actions.

## Product Purpose

Present RIANCOM SRL / Riancom '92 as a real manufacturer and supplier of construction materials, explain the available product families and transport service, and turn qualified interest into direct quote requests.

## Positioning

Riancom has operated since 1992, produces masonry blocks at its own unit in Răstoaca, Vrancea, maintains a physical location and real stock, and offers paid transport throughout Romania.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often comparing materials, sizes, approximate quantities, and delivery destinations. The main conversion paths are a phone call, WhatsApp conversation, email, map directions, or a prepared quote request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page presentation site in Romanian; no accounts, checkout, e-commerce, or prices.
- Product families: masonry blocks, foundation blocks, post blocks, paving, curbs, cement, sand, ballast, aggregates, and other construction materials.
- Masonry block sizes currently supplied by the brief: 10 × 20 × 40 cm and 15 × 20 × 40 cm with tongue-and-groove; 20 × 20 × 40 cm.
- Foundation block size currently supplied by the brief: H 20 × 20 × 50 cm.
- Post-block dimensions are unvalidated and must not be presented as final specifications.
- Blocks may be described cautiously as vibro-pressed and made from natural aggregates. Historical ingredient notes are not certifications.
- Transport is paid and priced by order and distance. Do not claim fleet size, capacity, tonnage, or guaranteed delivery times.
- No form backend exists. The quote form must prepare an honest contact handoff rather than simulate submission.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: RIANCOM or RIANCOM '92; legal name RIANCOM SRL.
- Romanian voice: direct, professional, accessible, and factual; no corporate jargon or unsupported superlatives.
- Industrial-modern, clean, restrained, credible, mobile-first visual character.
- Use a simple typographic wordmark if no strong logo asset exists.
- Prioritize authentic company photography and do not generate substitute imagery at this stage.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photographs in `poze/`: block products, production equipment, fresh production, stock, physical store/depot, aggregate transport, and Riancom '92 branded trucks.
- Confirmed contact details: Strada Brăilei nr. 92, 627208 Răstoaca, Vrancea, România; 0766 561 598; riancom92@yahoo.com.
- Confirmed hours: Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00; Saturday and Sunday closed.
- No validated testimonials, ratings, customer counts, employee counts, production capacity, certifications, prices, or technical performance claims are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Show real production before making claims about it.
- Keep direct contact available everywhere, especially on mobile.
- Make products and known dimensions easy to scan without implying unverified technical certainty.
- Optimize for fast understanding and fast loading.
- Keep content and image references centralized so future client validation and professional photo replacement are simple.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, explicit form labels, meaningful alternative text, high contrast, 44 px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow are required.
